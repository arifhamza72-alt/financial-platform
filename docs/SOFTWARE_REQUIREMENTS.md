# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## 1. Problem Statement
Millions of borrowers take loans without fully understanding their long-term financial impact. Most people only focus on the monthly EMI while ignoring the total interest paid over the loan's lifetime, the effect of prepayments, changes in floating interest rates, and alternative repayment strategies.

Our platform aims to help users visualize their financial future, understand how their loan behaves over time, and make informed decisions that reduce debt, save interest, and improve financial well-being.

## 2. Target Users
Primary Users
    Home Loan Borrowers
    Personal Loan Borrowers
    Car Loan Borrowers
These are the people using the application every day.

Secondary Users
    Financial Advisors
    Research Students
    Bank Employees

Future Enterprise Users
    Banks
    Financial Institutions

## 3. Vision
To become the most trusted financial decision-support platform that empowers borrowers to understand, optimize, and take control of their loans through accurate calculations, interactive visualizations, and intelligent financial insights.

## 6. Success Criteria
Loan calculations are mathematically accurate.
Users can compare repayment strategies.
Reports can be exported to PDF and Excel.
Application is responsive on desktop and mobile.
Loan optimization clearly demonstrates potential savings.
Results are easy to understand through charts and summaries.
